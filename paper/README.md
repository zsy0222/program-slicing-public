# Research evidence base

`paper/` is the authoritative working database for literature synthesis and research documentation.

- `matrix/`: the normalized literature matrix and evidence map.
- `notes/`: research questions, reading notes, screening criteria, and method revisions.
- `reports/`: proposal, process summary, experiment procedure, and literature audits.
- `scripts/`: self-authored generators for research documents.

Do not add copyrighted PDFs, downloaded publisher content, or unverified derivative translations to this directory. Keep citation metadata and concise research notes instead.
